Sacred Seasons is a web app for daily guided prayer, made by Dr. Julene Tegerstrand. It is meant to feel like having a spiritual director in your pocket. Each day from November 29 to Christmas Eve has one short prayer with Scripture and silence. The Advent prayers are free to use.

Would your church pray this Advent with Sacred Seasons?

Slowing down is a skill, and it does not just happen. When we practice listening prayer, we grow in stillness before God, and we become people who can listen to each other. The self-awareness we need with God is the same self-awareness we need in our hardest relationships.

If you use Sacred Seasons with your church or group, will you let Dr. Julene know? She would love to hear how it is going and how she can support you.

How a group can use it

  • Everyone prays on their own during the week: on a phone, in the car, on a walk with the dog, or at the kitchen table.
  • Once a week, the group meets to share how it is going and what is coming up in prayer.
  • Dr. Julene can provide leader guides for the weekly meeting and shape them to fit your group.
  • A free Church Kit already has bulletin inserts, slides, and a poster.
  • After a prayer, anyone can send a friend an e-card with a verse and a few words of encouragement.

Using it on your phone

Open Sacred Seasons in your phone's browser and save it to your home screen. It will open like an app. Dr. Julene is working on iOS and Android versions, and the App Store process has taken longer than she hoped. To become a tester when they are ready, or to hear about other seasons, sign up at sacredseasons.julenetegerstrand.com.
