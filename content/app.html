Sacred Seasons is a web app for daily guided prayer, made by Dr. Julene Tegerstrand. It is meant to feel like having a spiritual director in your pocket. Each day from November 29 to Christmas Eve has one short prayer with Scripture and silence. The Advent prayers are free to use.

Open Sacred Seasons

Pray with your church or group this Advent

Invite people to pray with the app each day during the week. Then gather once a week, on Zoom or in person, to listen to each other and share what God is doing in this season of preparation.

Two ways to gather

Way 1: Listen to each other

A weekly gathering of 60 or 90 minutes. Each person shares a short check-in while the group listens in silence. Then you talk together and pray for each other.

Way 2: Pray Lectio Divina together

About 25 minutes on one Gospel passage each week. The group hears it four times, with silence and sharing. There is a guide for each week.

Choose one way, or alternate. Both begin with each person praying with the app during the week.

Four steps for the person who organizes

  1. Choose your gathering. Pick one day and time each week, the same every week, on Zoom or in person. Advent runs November 29 to December 24.
  2. Invite people. Send the invitation with the app link about a week before Advent begins.
  3. Share the guide you chose. Give everyone the app link, and give the leader the guide for your way.
  4. Pray each day, gather each week. Anyone can lead, and the role can rotate.

Using it on your phone

Open Sacred Seasons in your phone's browser and save it to your home screen. It will open like an app. Dr. Julene is working on iOS and Android versions. To become a tester when they are ready, or to hear about other seasons, sign up at sacredseasons.julenetegerstrand.com.
