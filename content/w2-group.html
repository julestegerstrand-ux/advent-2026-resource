This week, we focus on the ministry of John the Baptist and the various ways that we are called to prepare for the coming of the Lord. The Advent theme is Peace, and as we engage with the Gospel we are reminded that one important aspect of peace is having peace with God. Peace with God is about being in right relationship with God, and this comes through repentance and forgiveness.
