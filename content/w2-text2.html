Psalm 85:1-2 and 8-13 is a song of thanksgiving and hope that celebrates God's forgiveness, peace, and the harmonious meeting of love, faithfulness, righteousness, and peace. It is this theme of peace that makes the Psalm a fitting one for the Second Sunday of Advent. Other Advent connections include the promise and hope that God's "salvation is near those who fear him," and that God's "glory may dwell in our land" (verse 9). The Psalm also affirms that righteousness goes before the Promise and prepares the way (verse 13). We find then, the Advent theme of preparation for the promise of peace, a peace that is connected to righteousness as God comes near.

The Psalm moves from remembering past forgiveness (verses 1–2) to listening for God's present word (verse 8) and culminating in a vision of divine attributes uniting (verses 10–13). During Advent, this psalm bridges the tension between what God has already done (incarnation/past deliverance) and what we still wait for (the final renewal of creation).

Key Themes

  • Forgiveness: God turns away anger and forgives the sins of the people.
  • Listening for Peace: The psalmist waits to hear God speak.
  • Divine Harmony: Verse 10 offers a famous poetic image where abstract virtues like love, faithfulness, righteousness, and peace come alive and embrace

Responsive Reading of Psalm 85

Outline of Psalm 85 from The Outline Bible

The psalmist seeks God in his need on the basis of God's past faithfulness to him. He expresses confidence that God will pour out blessings.

1. ACKNOWLEDGING THE PAST (85:1-3)

A. God restores his people (85:1): He blesses them.

B. God forgives his people (85:2-3): He covers their sins and ends his anger.

II. QUESTIONING THE PRESENT (85:4-7)

A. "Will you be angry with us always?" (85:4-6): They ask God to put aside his wrath against them and future generations.

B. "Show us your unfailing love, O LORD" (85:7): They ask God to grant them salvation.

III. ANTICIPATING THE FUTURE (85:8-13)

A. The glorious message (85:8-9): God promises peace and salvation to people who honor him.

B. The glorious meetings (85:10-13)

1. Unfailing love and truth meet (85:10).

2. Righteousness and peace kiss (85:10-13)
