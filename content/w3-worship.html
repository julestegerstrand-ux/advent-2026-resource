Worship on the Third Sunday of Advent

Also called Gaudete Sunday (Latin for "rejoice"), the Third Sunday of Advent focuses on Joy. We light the pink candle and rejoice in the approaching Advent of Jesus. We can include Advent songs and themes, but can lean into this "Christmas" theme of joy. Hard not to sing Joy to the World! :) (Other than the opening line, this hymn of the Kingdom of God actually has very little direct language of Advent/Christmas.)

Worship Themes to Consider

  • Joy
  • Rejoicing (Rejoicing in hope)
  • Praise
  • Joy as gift, resistance, anticipation

Advent Call to Worship

Leader: The world is a place of waiting, often shadowed by uncertainty and hardship. Yet, even in these times, a profound joy is offered to us.

People: We come together seeking that joy, to let it fill our hearts and overflow into the world.

Leader: For God's promise is near, and God's presence is with us. Let us lift our voices in joyful praise for the coming of the Lord.

People: Let us worship God with shouts of joy and open hearts!

Responsive Reading - Psalm 126

We can also use the Psalms reading as a call to worship, or elsewhere in the worship flow. Each week, we have the Psalm in the form of a responsive reading, which is a great option. It adds additional Scripture in our worship and also gives congregational participation in a way beyond singing.

You can also explore various worship resources connected to the Psalm on the Psalm 126 page.
