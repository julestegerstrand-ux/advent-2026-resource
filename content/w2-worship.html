Worship on the Second Sunday of Advent

This week, we continue to focus on Advent in general, and also introduce the theme of Peace. (As has been said elsewhere, sometimes the themes in Weeks Two and Four flip between Peace and Love. Different years, different traditions, and different text choices, among other factors, can all influence this. The themes of Weeks One and Three are mostly fixed.) The Scripture texts invite us to think of Peace in multiple dimensions. It is Peace (rest, calm, stillness) in our hearts. It is Peace (reconciliation, relationship, restoration) with God. It is Peace (reconciliation, relationship restoration) with others. In a world of conflict and division, this Advent theme is no small claim. In a season of rush and hurriedness, this Advent theme is no small feat. Invite people into all of the dimensions of Peace!

Note: With the theme of Peace, I am suggesting the use of some Christmas carols/songs with that theme. While generally we want to use them sparingly in Advent, I am suggesting that we limit the use of Christmas music to songs that highlight the weekly theme. Keep some true Advent-themed music present each week.

Worship Themes to Consider

  • Peace, rest, stillness
  • Reconciliation with God and others
  • Peacekeeping
  • Justice
  • Wholeness
  • Jesus as the Prince of Peace
  • The Shalom of God
  • The coming, peaceable Kingdom

Advent Call to Worship

Leader: We gather in these darkening days, anticipating the light that is to come, for we long for peace in a world filled with turmoil. Not merely the absence of conflict, but God's deep, abiding shalom—justice, harmony, and a deep sense of calm in our hearts.

All: We come preparing the way of the Lord, to make a highway in our spirits for God's peace to enter in.

Leader: We lift up our voices, a longing for a world where swords are beaten into plowshares, and our spirits are freed from anxiety and fear.

All: We seek the presence of God, the steady, unchanging character of the Prince of Peace, whose light guides our feet into ways of peace.

Leader: Let us be bearers of God's peace into our communities, our families, and the world, as we wait with hopeful hearts for the arrival of Christ, the Prince of Peace.

All: In the name of the One who is coming, bringing hope, love, peace, and joy, Amen.
