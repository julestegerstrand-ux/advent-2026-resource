Each week has Lectio Divina on four passages and two short practices. Pray them on your own or with a group. You do not need experience to lead them.

Week 2 Spiritual Practices PDF

This week we hear God speak tenderly to a weary people, and we look for one small road toward peace.

Lectio Divina

Isaiah 40:1-2, 10-11
Psalm 85:8-11
2 Peter 3:8-9, 13
Mark 1:5-8

Practices

At the Jordan (15 minutes)
The Kiss of Peace (20 minutes)

Each passage and practice starts on a new page, so you can print only the one you need. The handout comes as a ready-to-print PDF.
