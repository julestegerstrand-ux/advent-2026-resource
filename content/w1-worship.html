Worship on the First Sunday of Advent

The challenge in Advent worship is to let Advent be Advent. We often want to rush right into Christmas songs when we need to resist the urge and any pressure from the culture and even our people. It may be that we can crossfade and gradually add them in, but be sure and let this first Sunday of Advent focus on songs of Advent, songs of longing, expectancy, and hope.

Worship Themes to Consider

  • Anticipation: Focus on the waiting and expectation of Christ's arrival.
  • Light vs. Darkness: Contrast the darkness of the world with the coming light of Christ.
  • Prophetic Fulfillment: Connect the coming of Jesus to the fulfillment of Old Testament prophecies.
  • Readiness: Call the congregation to prepare their hearts to receive the Savior, much like the people of old.
  • The Dual Coming: Acknowledge that Christ came in humility and will come again in glory.

Advent Call to Worship

Leader: The night is passing. The day of salvation is near.
People: We are waiting for the dawn of God's incarnation.
Leader: Let us hear the revelation of God's promise.
People: For Advent is a time of expecting new light to shine.
Leader: Let us receive light from the prophets.
People: For light penetrates darkness and prepares the way of the Lord.
Leader: Let us see God's glory shine forth.
All: For we long to be transformed by the light of God's holiness. Come, Lord Jesus!

We can also use the Psalms reading as a call to worship, or elsewhere in the worship flow. Each week, we have the Psalm in the form of a responsive reading, which is a great option. It adds additional Scripture in our worship and also gives congregational participation in a way beyond singing.
