For a children's lesson on the first Sunday of Advent, focus on the theme of hope through the Advent wreath, the concept of God's promise of Jesus, and the importance of waiting and watching for His coming, both for the birth at Christmas and for His future return. Activities can include lighting the first purple candle on the Advent wreath, singing hymns, reading Bible passages like Jeremiah 33:14-16 or Luke 21:25-36, and creating a "Jesse tree" with ornaments representing God's plan.

Key Concepts for the First Sunday of Advent

  • Hope: This is the central theme for the first Sunday of Advent.
  • God's Promises: God promised to send a Savior, and He fulfilled that promise with Jesus' birth and will fulfill it again when Jesus returns.
  • Waiting and Watching: Advent is a season of waiting for Jesus, both at Christmas and for His second coming.

Activity Ideas

  1. The Advent Wreath and Prophecy Candle:
    • Introduce the Advent wreath and explain that the first purple candle represents hope.
    • Explain that this candle reminds us of the prophets, especially Isaiah, who foretold Jesus' birth.
    • Light the candle and discuss God's promise to send the "godly Branch" from David's royal line.
  2. Bible Stories and Readings:
    • Read from Jeremiah 33:14-16, which speaks of God's promises being fulfilled in the "godly Branch".
    • Consider using Luke 21:25-36 to discuss being watchful and ready for Jesus' return.
  3. The Jesse Tree:
    • Start a Jesse tree project by creating ornaments that represent biblical figures and events leading up to Jesus' birth.
    • For the first Sunday, you could make an ornament representing a prophet like Isaiah or a symbol of hope, like a star.
  4. Discussion and Reflection:
    • Ask children to wonder how people felt waiting for God's promise of a Savior to be fulfilled.
    • Discuss how they can prepare for Jesus by being loving, kind, and helpful.

Bringing it Together

  • Prayer:
    Close with a prayer for God to help them be patient and to show and share His love while they wait.
  • The Gift of Hope:
    Emphasize that Jesus is the greatest gift, and the hope we have in Him does not disappoint.
