Key Concepts & Themes

  • The Nativity Story:
    Focus on the events of Jesus' birth, with lessons on baby Jesus in the manger, the shepherds, and the wise men. Crafts can include making manger scenes or nativity ornaments.
  • Peace on Earth:
    Use the theme of the Prince of Peace to teach children about finding peace through Jesus, especially during the holiday season.
  • Light of the World:
    Explore how Jesus' arrival brought light to the world, using themes to help children feel brave and hopeful.
  • Christmas Around the World:
    Use this theme to teach about how Christmas is celebrated in different cultures.
  • The Shepherds and Angels:
    Focus on the story of the angels announcing Jesus' birth to the shepherds.

CHILDREN'S SERMONS and LESSONS

This lesson focuses on the escape to Egypt in Matthew 2:13-23 to teach children that God protects and guides them, even when life is scary or uncertain. Key takeaways include trusting God, listening for His guidance, and understanding that He has a plan and will always be with them. Activities can include creating a "Hope Box" to write down prayers and goals, or making a "Light of the World Lantern" to remind them of God's light and protection.
Lesson overview

  • Main theme: God protects and guides us, and we can trust Him even in difficult times.
  • Key scripture: Matthew 2:13-23, "The Escape to Egypt".
  • Core concepts:
    • God's protection: God warned Joseph in a dream to flee to Egypt to keep Jesus safe from King Herod.
    • God's guidance: An angel warned Joseph in a dream to leave Egypt and return to Israel when Herod was gone.
    • Trust: We can trust that God has a plan for us, even when we are scared or things are uncertain.
    • God is with us: God is always with us and promises to care for us.

Activity ideas

  • "Hidden in His Heart Hope Box":
    Decorate a small box to hold prayer requests and goals, reminding children to put their trust in God and talk to Him.
  • "Light of the World Lantern":
    Make a craft that represents Jesus as the light of the world, using a paper lantern and a safe light source inside. This can visually reinforce the idea of being protected by Christ's love.
  • Maze activity:
    Use a maze to represent life's challenges and God's ability to guide us through them. You can lead the children through a maze while they think about times they needed God's help.
  • "God is in Control" drawing:
    Have the children draw pictures showing times when God helped them or was with them in a scary situation.

Discussion questions

  • What are some things that can make us feel scared?
  • How did God help Joseph and Mary?
  • What are some ways God can guide us today?
  • What does it mean to trust God with our lives?

ACTIVITIES

Activities and games

  • Scavenger hunt: Hide small nativity-themed items like an angel or a star and give kids clues to find them all.
  • Bible Bingo: Use a simple bingo game with Christmas-themed squares.
  • "I Spy" game: Use a printable "I Spy" picture find game with a Christmas theme.
  • Christmas carols and skits: Have the children sing carols or perform a simple skit, acting out the Christmas story.
