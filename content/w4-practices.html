Each week has Lectio Divina on four passages and two short practices. Pray them on your own or with a group. You do not need experience to lead them.

Week 4 Spiritual Practices PDF

This week, we stand with Mary as she asks a profound question and says yes to God.

Lectio Divina

Luke 1:35-38
2 Samuel 7:5-7, 11
Luke 1:46b-49
Romans 16:25-27

Practices

The Wide View and the Small Room (20 minutes)
Keeping Silence with the Mystery (10 minutes)

Each passage and practice starts on a new page, so you can print only the one you need. The handout comes as a ready-to-print PDF.
