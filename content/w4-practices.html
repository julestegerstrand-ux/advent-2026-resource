This week’s handout gives you two ways to pray during Advent: Lectio Divina and short practices. You can pray each one on your own or with a small group, and you don’t need any experience to lead them. The steps are printed on each page.

Lectio Divina means sacred reading. It is an old way of praying with Scripture. You read a short passage aloud four times, slowly, and keep some silence after each reading. Each time, you listen for something different: a word that catches you, what it touches in your life, what you want to say to God, and then rest. In a group, a different person can read each time.

The practices are simple ways to pray with your body, your breath, and your attention. Each one takes 5 to 20 minutes and ends with questions to pray with or share.
