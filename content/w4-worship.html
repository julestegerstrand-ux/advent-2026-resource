Worship on the Fourth Sunday of Advent

On this final Sunday of Advent, our theme shifts to Love. We celebrate the gift of Love that is Jesus, and God's heart of love that gave it. We celebrate the completion of the Advent portion of our journey as we begin to transition to Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and the First Sunday of Christmastide as we proclaim the wonder of Christ's coming. We have been waiting in wonder; now we receive with that same posture of wonder and amazement.

Worship Themes to Consider

  • God's incarnational Love
  • Our love for God
  • Completion / the end of the wait
  • Emmanuel, God with us
  • Living the promise of the hope, peace, joy, and love of Christ's coming

Advent Call to Worship

Leader: The fourth week of Advent, the week of love, is upon us. Today we celebrate the steadfast love of God, who sent Jesus, a gift of perfect love, into the world to save us.

All: We, the people of God, respond to this divine love with our own love and gratitude.
We come to worship, ready to open our hearts to receive the love of Christ.

Leader: We will light the four Advent candles today, symbolizing our hope, peace, joy, and now, our deepest love.
May God's love fill us.

All: In the spirit of love, we are called to be a beacon of hope, peace, joy, and love to the world.
We are ready to celebrate and share God's love.

Leader: Let us worship God, who is love

We can also use the Psalms reading as a call to worship, or elsewhere in the worship flow. Each week, we have the Psalm in the form of a responsive reading, which is a great option. It adds additional Scripture in our worship and also gives congregational participation in a way beyond singing.
